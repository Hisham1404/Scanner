What gives them away, in general

  • Power. A camera runs on mains or a battery. Battery cameras die in hours, so anything meant to run for a shift is plugged in. Count the things plugged in and find the one nobody can explain.
  • A hole with no purpose. A pinhole 1–3 mm across on a face with no grille, no screw and no LED. Fresh drill dust beneath it, fresh filler around it, or a bead of silicone.
  • Line of sight. Every camera has to be able to see the thing it was put there to see. Stand where you would undress and look back at every object that can see you. That narrows a room to a handful of objects very quickly.
  • Heat. Continuous recording makes a device warm. Touch the back of anything you are unsure about.
  • Objects out of context. A charger in a bathroom. A clock facing the bed instead of the door. A dustbin with wiring. A smoke detector in a room with no others. This is what actually catches them.
  • Newness. One bright screw among tarnished ones, disturbed paint, a switchboard cleaner than the wall around it.

Why most detector apps do not work

Worth knowing, because you will be offered plenty of them.

Magnetometer apps. They read the phone’s compass and call any disturbance a camera. A published test had one flag 47 separate anomalies in a single hotel room, none of which were cameras. Wiring in walls, pipes, hinges, door frames and speakers all set it off. This tool deliberately has no magnetometer mode.

Infrared modes. Real physics — night-vision cameras do emit infrared at 850 or 940 nm — but modern phone cameras have strong infrared-cut filters over the sensor. Measured detection rates on current Samsung and Apple flagships fall below 10%. It is included here as a third mode with that caveat attached, not as a test to rely on.

What is left. Lens retroreflection, which has published support behind it, and network scanning, which works well but needs a native app. Everything else being sold is noise.

DeWiCam, AsiaCCS 2018, and later traffic-pattern work — wireless cameras are identifiable from traffic shape even when the payload is encrypted. Case details are from reported Indian news coverage.
