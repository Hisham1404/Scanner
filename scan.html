How to hold it
01

Kill the lights and draw the curtains

The scanner reads the difference the torch makes. The darker the room, the larger that difference is.

02

Hold the phone at arm’s length, screen towards you

The torch has to be beside the lens you are looking through — that is the whole trick. Watch the screen, not the room.

03

Go slowly — about three seconds per metre

A candidate has to be seen across several detection cycles before it scores highly, so sweeping fast defeats the thing that makes this reliable.

04

Stand one to three metres back

Too close and the beam overshoots; too far and a lens is too few pixels to resolve. Use zoom rather than walking closer.

05

Cross every suspect spot twice, from different angles

A chrome tap throws a highlight that slides away and dies when you move. A lens keeps returning light to you. That difference is the real test, and it is yours to make, not the app’s.

06

Sweep the ceiling as its own pass

Then above eye level, then waist height. Do the ceiling with nothing else on your mind.

Reading the result
70–100Something is bouncing light straight back at you. Go and look at that spot with your hands.
48–69A reflector of some kind. Could be a lens, could be chrome, glass or a polished screw head. Re-cross it in Pulse mode from a different angle.
0–47Ordinary shiny surface. Keep sweeping.
What this will flag that is not a camera

Polished taps and shower fittings, mirror edges, glass and spectacle lenses, screw heads, smooth tile, screens, glossy paint, foil, animal eyes, and reflective thread in curtains. Retroreflection is a property of a lens, not a property unique to cameras. The two-angle test above is what separates them — do it every time.

Do not go straight to confrontation

If you find something in someone else’s property, the next page you want is Legal, not the reception desk. There is a right order for this, and getting it wrong destroys the evidence.

Scanner runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you scan, flag or write is uploaded — there is no server, no account, no analytics. Findings live in this browser’s local storage and clearing site data erases them.

A search aid, not a guarantee. A clean sweep means you did not find a camera, which is not the same as there not being one. Nothing here is legal advice.
