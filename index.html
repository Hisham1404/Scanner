How it works

1 · Lens-glint scan

A lens focused onto a sensor bounces light straight back at its source. Your torch sits right beside your camera, so a hidden lens lights up as a small bright dot. The scanner strobes the torch and analyses only the light the torch put there, which cancels out room lights, standby LEDs and windows.

2 · Guided sweep

Seven zones, walked in the order that actually finds things: bathroom first, then mirrors and changing areas, then the bed, then everything above head height. Each checkpoint names the specific spot and what the tell looks like.

3 · Know what you are looking for

    What this tool can and cannot do

    Most “hidden camera detector” apps are not honest about this, so here it is plainly.

    MethodWorks?Why
    Lens retroreflection Yes The one method that reaches every device type, including offline SD-card cameras. Published work (LAPD, ACM SenSys 2021) measured 88.9% detection this way against 46% for the naked eye.
    Physical search Yes Unglamorous and effective. In nearly every case in the guide, a person found the camera by noticing something out of place.
    Infrared LED spotting Barely Night-vision cameras emit infrared, but flagship phone cameras filter it out. Measured detection rates on current Samsung and Apple flagships are under 10%. Included as a bonus mode, not a test you can rely on.
    Magnetometer “detectors” No Deliberately left out. One published test had a magnetometer app flag 47 anomalies in a single hotel room, none of which were cameras. It reacts to wiring, pipes and hinges.
    Wi-Fi and network scanning Not in a browser The strongest automated signal for networked cameras, and no website can do it on any browser. Scanning Wi-Fi, listing devices on a network and reading MAC addresses all need a native app. This is the main reason for building one.
    Your phone is a good instrument for this A bright torch mounted next to a high-resolution camera with optical zoom is genuinely the right hardware for retroreflection work. What it is not is a radio scanner — that part needs the app.

    Scanner runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you scan, flag or write is uploaded — there is no server, no account and no analytics. Your findings live in this browser’s local storage and clearing site data erases them.
