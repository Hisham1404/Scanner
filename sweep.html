Tick means checked, not safe The tick is a memory aid so you do not lose your place halfway through a room at midnight. Anything you mark with the warning sign goes into the report with a timestamp.

Three things worth doing before you start

  • Do the sweep before you unpack, while the room is still exactly as it was handed to you. A room you have lived in for an hour is much harder to read.
  • Photograph the room on arrival, wide shots from each corner. If you find something later, you will want to show what was where at the start.
  • Trust the thing that feels slightly wrong. In almost every reported case in the guide, somebody found the camera because an object was somewhere it had no business being — wiring on a dustbin, a phone left face-down, a smoke detector aimed at a bed.

Scanner runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you scan, flag or write is uploaded — there is no server, no account and no analytics. Your findings live in this browser’s local storage and clearing site data erases them.

This is a search aid, not a guarantee. A clean sweep means you did not find a camera, which is not the same as there not being one. Nothing here is legal advice.
