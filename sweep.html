Before you start
01

Sweep before you unpack

Do it while the room is still exactly as it was handed to you. A room you have lived in for an hour is much harder to read.

02

Photograph the room on arrival

Wide shots from each corner. If you find something later, you will want to show what was where at the start.

03

Trust the thing that feels slightly wrong

In almost every reported case in the guide, somebody found the camera because an object was somewhere it had no business being — wiring on a dustbin, a phone left face-down, a smoke detector aimed at a bed.

Marking

Clear means checked, not safe — it is a memory aid so you do not lose your place halfway through a room at midnight. Flag puts the checkpoint into the report with a timestamp.

Scanner runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you scan, flag or write is uploaded — there is no server, no account, no analytics. Findings live in this browser’s local storage and clearing site data erases them.

A search aid, not a guarantee. A clean sweep means you did not find a camera, which is not the same as there not being one. Nothing here is legal advice.
